Get help

Email support@wickedapi.com, or open an issue at github.com/WickedAPI/wicked-brain/issues.

Please include your operating system, which agent you use (Codex, Claude Code, or Antigravity), the plugin version, and what you expected to happen. Please never paste tokens, passphrases, or private notes.

Common questions

  • Search says no vault is set. Run the brain-setup skill, or set WICKED_BRAIN_VAULT_PATH to your vault folder.
  • The plugin does not start. It needs uv installed and on your PATH. Restart the app after installing it.
  • Is my data sent to you? No. See the Privacy Policy.
